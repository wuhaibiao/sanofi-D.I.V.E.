# 答辩演示脚本：memsys chat --user demo --clock sim --at "2026-10-01 09:00" --script docs/demo.txt
# 用模拟时钟在几分钟里走完几个月，每一段对应一项能力。以 # 开头的行和空行会被跳过。

# 1. 积累：随口提到的个人信息会被抽取成事实
I just started a new job as a backend engineer at a game studio in Hangzhou. Can you suggest a good mechanical keyboard for long coding days?
By the way, I'm allergic to peanuts, so keep that in mind if you ever suggest snacks.
/end
/mem

# 2. 更新：情况变了，旧事实被取代但保留在版本链里
/time +6w
I switched jobs last month, now I work at a brokerage in Shanghai. Any tips for my commute on the metro?
/end
/hist job

/end
